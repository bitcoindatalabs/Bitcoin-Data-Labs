Research

Research papers, with the code and data behind every number.

Each paper takes one question about how Bitcoin and its payment networks behave and answers it from public data. The preprints are on arXiv, and every figure, table and number in them is produced by code published alongside.

How we do research

  1. Every number comes from code

    Each figure, table and quoted number is written by the pipeline in the paper's repository, from a fixed data cutoff. Nothing is typed in by hand.

  2. Aggregates only

    Security results are published as totals. No list, ranking or label of individual nodes by software release or exposure appears in a paper or its data, and we don't provide one on request.

  3. Public inputs

    The papers read the public Lightning gossip graph and the Bitcoin blockchain. Our daily snapshots of them are available to researchers on request.

  4. Measure, don't advocate

    We report what the record shows about clients, operators and maintainers, including when it is not what anyone expected.
