The problem

Bitcoin is public. Its story isn't.

Bitcoin has no CEO, no board and no earnings call. Everything about it is on the record, but nobody reads that record and reports on it.

Scattered

The record is spread across GitHub, mailing lists, IRC, Lightning gossip and the chain itself.

Unmeasured

The software most of the network runs has no published numbers on review load, maintainer risk or who funds the work.

Argued, not checked

Debates about clients, centralization and funding run on opinion because the data takes weeks to assemble.

Our answer

TransparencyBring the scattered record into open dashboards and data anyone can check.
AccountabilityShow where development effort and funding actually go.
ForesightSpot shifts in code, clients and capital before they become debates.

How we work · How you can help

Keep Bitcoin's public record free.

  • Open by defaultOpen-source code, public data, no paywalls or logins.
  • ReproducibleEvery number traces to a public pipeline and a methodology page.
  • Honest about gapsMissing data shows as "unavailable". We never fill gaps with a guess.
  • Measure, don't advocateNo token, no sides. Just the record.
